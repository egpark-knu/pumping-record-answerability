## Author-fixed mechanical decision

For P2_current_vs_1p5x at independent lead10, let R_i^original, R_i^range and R_i^control denote signed engine/truth ratios for the same original record. Compute d_range=median_i(R_i^range−R_i^original) and d_control=median_i(R_i^control−R_i^original), with all1,242 paired records included. These are medians of paired differences, not differences of medians, absolute effects, ratios of pooled sums or per-realization medians.

Condition(a): the95% realization-unit bootstrap percentile interval for d_range is strictly above0 (lower endpoint>0). A lower endpoint equal0 fails. Condition(b): d_range>=2*d_control, using the same point median change and ordinary signed arithmetic. Equality passes(b). A negative or zero control difference is used as written and its value and doubled value are disclosed; no absolute value, minimum-effect cutoff, positivity floor, alternative branch or reinterpretation is allowed. Support iff(a) and(b), otherwise unsupported. The output must expose a_interval_lower, a_interval_upper, treatment_median_change, control_median_change, doubled_control_median_change, condition_a, condition_b and mechanical_decision. Incomplete data prevent a valid full-cohort criterion, rather than being classified as unsupported scientific evidence.
