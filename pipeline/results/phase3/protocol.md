## A: independent count and recency placement, daily boundary convention
Origin is the boundary at index 1024. Burst end b is the first ON day after the final short OFF day; b=1024-round(f*t95), with f=.25 recent or 2 old (Python round, same rule as D03). An N-day alternating template [b-N,b) is ON on its first day and OFF on its odd days; the last OFF is [b-1,b). The actual final on/off switch is at boundary b, so its recency is exactly round(f*t95) days. Nominal/realized recency and final OFF index are saved. There are exactly N on/off switches attributable to the short burst, N/2 one-day OFF intervals and N/2 prescribed one-day ON intervals. Because this burst is detached from the long rest, its first prescribed ON day joins the preceding long ON run: the actual maximal-run census is N/2 OFF and N/2-1 interior short ON runs (N-1 interior short runs). These distinct counts are disclosed; N is the actual burst switch count, never the maximal-run census. All OFF events remain one day and strictly outside the designated rest. This boundary consequence is not hidden by assigning the long rest to the burst.

The common designated rest end e for all four cells in a layer/realization is min(b-N over all four cells)-ceil(t95); R=round(t95). Thus the gap from rest to earliest burst is at least t95. The rest position/length, weather, noise, hidden prehistory Qpre=100 and final q0 are identical across all four cells. Compensation window [0,e-R) is common. Like D03, kappa=1+sum(base0[short OFF])/sum(base0[compensation]), base0=100*pilot multiplier, then designated rest and short OFF are zero. Therefore total context volume and mean over all days outside designated rest match across all four cells; no N/recency-dependent rest timing is introduced. No requirement is imposed on the postburst tail exceeding t95: recent placement explicitly requires a shorter tail.
